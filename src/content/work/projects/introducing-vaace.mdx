## Overview

Team V.A.A.C.E is pleased to announce it's 5 figure funding from NASA.

## Project Progress

- **Successful Proposal**: Our team developed a novel phase-change balloon system to support Venus exploration. We’ve made exciting progress in prototyping and this is only the beginning!
- **Funding**: Awarded $10,000 from NASA through NPWEE to support further development.
- **Upcoming Experiments**: Two mission-critical experiments scheduled for June–July 2025 to validate our system under simulated atmospheric conditions.

## Team Expertise
Our work brought together multidisciplinary strengths in:

- **Engineering & Design**:CAD modeling, pressure systems prototyping, and mechanical systems integration.
- **Software & Analysis**: Programming in C++, Python, and MATLAB for data collection and control simulations.
- **Collaboration & Outreach**: Engaging experts for feedback and partnering with manufacturers to bring designs to life.

## Member Highlights

- Bartek Broclawik, a junior at NJIT, says "the project was complex from the start, but applying systems analysis helped us cut through the noise and move with purpose. My work on the lab inflation test—from schematic design to procurement—reflected real-world electrical engineering practices."
- Vamsi Kurakalva, a Masters Student at ASU states "being on this team has deepened my drive to explore the cosmos through human ingenuity, inspired by the pioneering Venus probes of both NASA and the Soviet Venera missions."
- Project Manager and Rutgers student, Sneha Sahadevan, describes her experience so far as “an incredible opportunity to work with wonderful and exceptional students from around the nation to advance aerospace technology.”
