## Overview

Hello Earth (and Hello Venus)!
Team V.A.A.C.E is thrilled to announce that, as part of Phase One of our project, on the 10th of August 2025, we successfully completed a payload test by launching our first prototype high-altitude aerobot to 80 km and retrieving valuable data under extreme low-temperature and low-pressure conditions.

## Project Progress
We extend our heartfelt thanks to L’SPACE NASA NPWEE Program for their support and funding, to the University of Maryland's Balloon Payload Program (BPP) for hosting, and to our incredible advisors and teammates whose dedication, countless hours, and perseverance carried us through technical challenges to this milestone.
From the summer of 2024 to today, we’ve been working toward a novel approach to Venus exploration. This milestone marks just the beginning.

## New Announcement

- We’re equally excited to share that, with continued NASA supportsponsorship, we are entering Phase Two. In this next stage, we’ll be expanding our team and scaling up our next aerobot iteration.

Stay updated with our latest news by following us on LinkedIn and Instagram (links in the footer). Thank you for supporting our journey to explore Venus in new ways!
